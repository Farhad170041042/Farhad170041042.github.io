About

Research-driven teaching and language AI

I am a Lecturer in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Green University of Bangladesh. I received my B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering from Islamic University of Technology (IUT), where my undergraduate thesis focused on ChartSumm, a benchmark for automatic chart summarization.

My research interests are centered on Natural Language Processing, especially language generation from structured and visual data, logical reasoning, natural language inference, question answering, and the evaluation of modern transformer and large language models. I enjoy turning research ideas into reproducible systems, datasets, and student-led projects.
